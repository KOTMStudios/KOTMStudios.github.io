Privacy Policy

Idle Rift RPG · KOTM Studios · Last updated 16 September 2026

Idle Rift RPG does not collect personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no advertising. From version 0.8.8 it sends anonymous usage data and crash reports to help us fix and balance the game, and you can switch that off at any time in Settings.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. The name you give your character stays on your device and is never sent. We do not use your device's advertising identifier: the game switches its collection off and does not request the permission to read it.

To understand how the game is played and to fix problems, the app uses two Google Firebase services, which receive the following:

  • Usage data (Firebase Analytics). Game events such as creating a character, reading a tutorial card, starting and finishing a rift, defeating a boss, reaching a level or a vocation, using the forge, opening a chest and resetting through prestige, with game details such as the zone, difficulty and level. Firebase also records when the app is opened and for how long, together with the device model, operating system version, app version, language, and an approximate country or region derived from the connection.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). When the app crashes or hits an error: the technical details of the error, the device model and operating system version, the app version, basic device state such as free memory, and the last few game events before the problem.

Both services identify an installation of the app with a random identifier that Firebase creates on your device. It is not linked to your name, your Google account, or any account of ours.

How we use it

Only to find and fix crashes, and to learn where the game is confusing, too hard or too slow — for example, how many players finish the tutorial or reach their first boss. We do not sell this data, do not use it for advertising, and do not combine it with information from other sources.

Switching it off

Open the menu (☰), then ⚙ Settings, and turn off Share usage data and crash reports. While it is off the game sends no usage data and no crash reports, and Firebase collection is disabled on your device. You can turn it back on the same way. The game plays exactly the same either way.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress is saved locally on your device so the game can continue where you left off. That save contains only game state:

  • Your character's name, class, experience, level, and weapon skills
  • Items, materials, gold, and potions you are carrying
  • Which zones you have unlocked and which creatures you have studied
  • Your game settings, such as potion thresholds
  • A randomly generated character identifier, created on your device

The save never leaves your device. It is held in your device's private app storage, where no other app can read it and neither can we.

Third parties

Usage data and crash reports are processed by Google as a service provider for Firebase, under the Firebase privacy and security terms and Google's privacy policy. The game contains no advertising networks and no social media integrations.

If you installed the game through Google Play, Google may also collect information about the installation itself under its own privacy policy. That is a function of the store, not of this game.

How long it is kept

Crash reports are kept for 90 days. Usage data is kept for no longer than 14 months, after which it is deleted automatically.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age. There is nothing in it that would allow a child to disclose personal information: no chat, no user profiles, no user-generated content that is sent anywhere, and no way to contact other players.

Deleting your data

Your game progress is entirely on your device:

  • Use the in-game wipe option to erase your character and start fresh
  • Clear the app's storage from your device's Settings
  • Uninstall the app, which removes the save entirely

Usage data and crash reports are not linked to anything that identifies you, so we cannot look them up for a specific person; they are deleted automatically as described above. If you have a question about them, write to us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes what data it handles — cloud saves or leaderboards, for example — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the release notes. The date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
