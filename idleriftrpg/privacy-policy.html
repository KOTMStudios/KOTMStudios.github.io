Privacy Policy

Idle Rift RPG · KOTM Studios · Last updated 12 October 2026

Idle Rift RPG does not collect your name, email or any other personal information. The game has no accounts of its own. It sends anonymous usage data and crash reports to help us fix and balance the game, and you can switch that off in Settings. You can choose to sign in with Google Play Games to back up your characters and appear on the leaderboards. From version 0.10.0 the game offers optional rewarded ads, which you only see when you tap to watch one, and optional in-app purchases through Google Play. From version 0.10.6 you can choose to share the world with other players: your fallen heroes' graves and your feats, under your character's name. It is off until you accept its terms in the game.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. The name you give your character stays on your device, with two exceptions you choose: if you are signed in to Google Play Games, a Primordia character's name and vocation are published with its leaderboard scores, and if you turn on Show me to other players, they appear on your graves and in the chronicle (both described below). Do not use your real name as a character name if you do not want it shown.

To understand how the game is played and to fix problems, the app uses two Google Firebase services, which receive the following:

  • Usage data (Firebase Analytics). Game events such as creating a character, reading a tutorial card, starting and finishing a rift, defeating a boss, reaching a level or a vocation, using the forge, opening a chest, resetting through prestige, and using the in-game shop (which products were viewed and bought, how many Rift Tokens were earned or spent, whether a rewarded ad was watched), with game details such as the zone, difficulty and level. Firebase also records when the app is opened and for how long, together with the device model, operating system version, app version, language, and an approximate country or region derived from the connection.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). When the app crashes or hits an error: the technical details of the error, the device model and operating system version, the app version, basic device state such as free memory, and the last few game events before the problem.

Both services identify an installation of the app with a random identifier that Firebase creates on your device. It is not linked to your name, your Google account, or any account of ours. Firebase Analytics does not collect your device's advertising identifier.

Rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you tap a button to watch one in exchange for a boost (for example, extra experience for thirty minutes). No ad is ever shown unless you ask for it, and there are no banner or interstitial ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob.

  • What AdMob receives: your device's advertising identifier, the app and device information needed to serve and measure an ad (device model, operating system, language, approximate location from the connection), and whether the ad was watched to the end.
  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your choice through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change that choice at any time in ⚙ Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Content: ads are limited to content suitable for teenagers (rating T) and above.
  • Your advertising identifier: you can reset it, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).

Rift Patron subscribers receive the same rewards without watching ads.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The game sells optional in-app items (Rift Tokens, a starter pack) and an optional monthly subscription (Rift Patron). All payments are handled by Google Play: we never see your card or payment details.

  • What the game receives from Google Play: for each purchase, its order number, a purchase token, the product and the purchase time, and for the subscription, whether it is currently active. The game checks the purchase's signature on your device and then records it.
  • Where it is kept: on your device, and in your cloud backup if you use one, so that Rift Tokens you bought are not lost if you change phones. We run no server that receives it.
  • Cancelling the subscription: at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. The game links there from the Rift Patron screen.

Cloud save and leaderboards (Google Play Games)

The version of the game installed from Google Play can use Google Play Games. It only does so if you are signed in to Play Games, and signing in is your choice.

  • Cloud save: the same game state described under “What the game stores on your device” below, for every character on the device, the shared stashes and your Rift Tokens and purchases, is backed up into the saved-games storage of your Google account. We run no server that receives it and cannot read it; only the game, signed in as you, can.
  • Leaderboards: characters in the world Primordia submit their totals to public leaderboards: total experience, gold earned, the longest single rift survived, the experience of each weapon skill and of Mining and Smithing, and bars smelted. Each score carries the character's name and vocation, which the game shows on its own Rankings screen. A leaderboard also shows your Play Games display name and avatar next to your score, to other players. You control who can see your Play Games profile in the Play Games app. To keep a character off the leaderboards, play it without signing in to Play Games.
  • What the game sees of your account: your Play Games display name, shown on your own screen to confirm which account holds the backup. It is not sent anywhere else by the game.

The game never replaces a cloud backup with the progress on a device without asking you first when the two differ.

Other players: graves and the chronicle (optional)

If you turn on Show me to other players (it is off until you accept its terms in the game), characters in the world Primordia share a little of their story with other players, through Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore):

  • Graves: when one of your characters dies in a rift, its name, vocation, level, what killed it, the zone and difficulty and the time are stored, and shown as a grave to other players in that zone.
  • The chronicle: a few feats the game itself announces — reaching level 50, 75 or 99, a prestige, a zone's boss on Normal or harder, a rift boss, a mythic item, Nemesis of a creature — with the character's name, vocation and level. You never type anything into it.
  • Respects and reports: when you pay respects at another player's grave, or report a name or a feat, that is stored with your anonymous identifier. Reports are read only by us, to act on them.
  • Your identifier: a random, anonymous Firebase Authentication identifier created on your device. It is not linked to your name, email, Google account or any account of ours.

Community rules: no offensive, hateful or impersonating names (a name the game's filter rejects is shown to nobody), no attempts to write anything the game would not. Players can report a name or a feat and block a player from their card; we review reports by hand and may remove what breaks these rules and stop an identifier from sharing again. Nothing shared pays gold, items or Rift Tokens.

To stop sharing, turn the switch off in ⚙ Settings → Privacy. To delete what you shared — your graves, your feats, your respects and the anonymous identifier — use Delete my social data in the same place. If you lose access to the game before doing so (for example by clearing its data), write to us at the address below with the character's name and we will delete it.

How we use it

Usage data and crash reports: only to find and fix crashes, and to learn where the game is confusing, too hard or too slow — for example, how many players finish the tutorial or reach their first boss. We do not sell this data, do not use it for advertising, and do not combine it with information from other sources. Purchase records: only to give you what you bought.

Switching it off

Open the menu (☰), then ⚙ Settings, and turn off Share usage data and crash reports. While it is off the game sends no usage data and no crash reports, and Firebase collection is disabled on your device. You can turn it back on the same way. The game plays exactly the same either way. Ads are only requested when you tap to watch one; if you never do, no ad is shown.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress is saved locally on your device so the game can continue where you left off. That save contains only game state:

  • Your character's name, class, experience, level, and weapon skills
  • Items, materials, gold, and potions you are carrying
  • Which zones you have unlocked and which creatures you have studied
  • Your game settings, such as potion thresholds
  • A randomly generated character identifier, created on your device
  • Your Rift Tokens, what you bought with them, your purchase records (order numbers and purchase tokens), the daily-track progress, active boosts and how many rewarded ads you watched today

The save is held in your device's private app storage, where no other app can read it and neither can we. It only leaves your device as the optional cloud backup described above.

Third parties

Usage data and crash reports are processed by Google as a service provider for Firebase, under the Firebase privacy and security terms. Rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob, which processes data as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Purchases are processed by Google Play, and the cloud save and leaderboards by Google Play Games, all under Google's privacy policy. The optional graves and chronicle are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, in the United States) under the same Firebase terms. The game contains no other advertising networks and no social media integrations.

If you installed the game through Google Play, Google may also collect information about the installation itself under its own privacy policy. That is a function of the store, not of this game.

How long it is kept

Crash reports are kept for 90 days. Usage data is kept for no longer than 14 months, after which it is deleted automatically. Purchase records stay in your save for as long as the save exists, because they are what proves a purchase was already delivered. Graves, feats and respects stay until you delete them (above); reports are kept only as long as we need them to act.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age. There is no chat, no user profiles and no way to contact other players. The only thing another player can see is a character's name with its game feats, and only after the player chose to share them. Ads are limited to content suitable for teenagers, and purchases require the Google Play account holder's payment method.

Deleting your data

Your game progress is on your device, and in your Google account if you use the cloud save:

  • Delete a character from the character screen to erase it
  • Clear the app's storage from your device's Settings
  • Uninstall the app, which removes the save from the device
  • Delete the game's saved data from your Google Play Games account in the Play Games settings, which removes the cloud backup and your leaderboard scores
  • Reset your advertising identifier in your device's settings
  • Use ⚙ Settings → Privacy → Delete my social data to remove your graves, feats and respects from the shared world

Usage data and crash reports are not linked to anything that identifies you, so we cannot look them up for a specific person; they are deleted automatically as described above. If you have a question about them, write to us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes what data it handles — an online market, for example — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the release notes. The date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.
